Privacy Policy & GCC Data Governance

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview and Core Privacy Principle

At GulfCalc (gulfcalc.tech), accessible from https://gulfcalc.tech, the privacy of our visitors is a fundamental architectural commitment. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that may be gathered when visiting GulfCalc and explains how client-side privacy safeguards your data, consistent with general data protection principles including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), and the UAE's Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection.

2. Client-Side Financial Calculation Isolation

Unlike web services that process financial inputs on remote application servers, GulfCalc does not collect, transmit, store, or profile your personal financial calculations.

Every numerical figure you input into our calculation engines (including basic salaries, housing allowances, transport allowances, years of service, and corporate revenues) is processed strictly within your device's local web browser JavaScript environment. No calculator inputs are transmitted over the network or saved in remote databases. Once you close or reload your browser tab, all calculated numbers are cleared from your device's memory.

3. Standard Web Server Log Files

Like standard static web properties delivered through edge networks (such as Cloudflare Pages), GulfCalc follows standard industry logging procedures. The information recorded by hosting logs may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser user-agent types, referring/exit pages, date and time stamps, and basic connection diagnostics. These log entries are not linked to personally identifiable information and are used solely for infrastructure diagnostics, security analysis, and service availability.

4. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising Partners (Google AdSense)

GulfCalc may display advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, including Google LLC:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant advertisements to users based on their visits to GulfCalc and other sites across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Your numerical calculator inputs are never shared with advertising partners or ad networks.

5. User Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

We respect user data rights regarding privacy, rectification, and access:

  • No Account Creation: GulfCalc does not require user accounts, logins, or registration.
  • No Personal Profiles: We do not construct user dossiers, credit profiles, or employment tracking records.
  • Right to Erasure / Access: Because all calculation data is held locally on your device and never stored on our infrastructure, there is no remote user calculation data to retrieve or erase.

6. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions or require further clarification regarding our privacy practices, you can contact the project founder Sheharyar Siraj via sheharyarsiraj.com or submit an inquiry through our public GitHub repository at github.com/Sheharyar-ans/gulfcalc.
