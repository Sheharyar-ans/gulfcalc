Understanding Kuwait End of Service Indemnity Under Law No. 6 of 2010

In the State of Kuwait, expatriate and private-sector terminal benefits are governed by Kuwait Private Sector Labour Law No. 6 of 2010. Commonly referred to as the Indemnity or Mukafoat Nihayat Al-Khidma, this statutory severance pay protects workers by establishing a mandatory financial accrual based on their length of uninterrupted service and final remuneration.

The Critical 26 Working Days Statutory Divisor (Article 55)

The single most frequent mistake committed by non-compliant payroll departments and generic financial calculators is dividing an employee's monthly remuneration by 30 calendar days. Article 55 of Law No. 6/2010 unambiguously mandates:

Statutory Daily Wage Formula (Kuwait Law No. 6/2010, Art. 55)
Daily Wage = Total Monthly Remuneration / 26 Working Days

Because Kuwait law treats weekly rest days as non-working days for indemnity computation purposes, dividing by 26 results in a higher, legally correct daily rate. An employee earning 1,300 KWD has a statutory daily rate of 50.000 KWD (1,300 / 26), whereas an unlawful 30-day divisor yields only 43.333 KWD, depriving the worker of 13.3% of their lawful severance.

Kuwait Indemnity Accrual Tiers and Statutory 1.5-Year Cap

Under Article 51 of the Labour Law, indemnity accumulates in two statutory tiers:

  • First 5 Years of Continuous Service: 15 working days' remuneration for each completed year.
  • Service Exceeding 5 Years: One full month (30 working days' remuneration) for each subsequent year.
  • Maximum Ceiling Cap: The total aggregate benefit payable under all tiers combined cannot exceed one and a half years' remuneration (18 months' salary).

Statutory Resignation Deduction Scale (Article 53)

When an employee working under an indefinite term contract voluntarily tenders their resignation, the full indemnity is reduced according to the statutory tenure tiers outlined in Article 53:

Continuous Service Period Resignation Entitlement (%) Applicable Legal Provision
Under 3 Completed Years 0% (No entitlement) Article 53, Kuwait Labour Law
3 Years to under 5 Years 50% (Half entitlement) Article 53, Kuwait Labour Law
5 Years to under 10 Years 66.67% (Two-thirds entitlement) Article 53, Kuwait Labour Law
10 or more Completed Years 100% (Full entitlement) Article 53, Kuwait Labour Law

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What components of compensation are included in the indemnity calculation?
Under Article 55, calculation is based on the worker's latest drawn remuneration, which encompasses basic salary plus all regular, guaranteed contractual allowances (such as fixed housing, transportation, and cost-of-living allowances). Discretionary bonuses and unquantified commissions are typically excluded.
Does an employee forfeit indemnity if dismissed?
Indemnity is only forfeited in rare situations of summary dismissal under Article 41 for gross misconduct (e.g., criminal conviction, severe financial damage to the employer, or physical assault). Standard dismissal or redundancy entitles the worker to 100% full indemnity.
Are partial years calculated pro-rata?
Yes. Article 51 stipulates that workers are entitled to a proportionate fraction for fractions of the year served, calculated accurately down to completed months and days.
