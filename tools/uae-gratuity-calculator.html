UAE Gratuity Calculator: Federal Decree-Law No. 33/2021 Unified Formula

Direct Legal Rule: Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33/2021 (Article 51), gratuity is calculated on last drawn basic salary: 21 calendar days per year for the first 5 years, and 30 days per year thereafter. Resignation and termination receive identical 100% treatment once 1 continuous year is completed, subject to a 2-year salary maximum cap.

The New UAE Labour Law: Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021

Effective February 2, 2022, Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 entirely replaced the previous 1980 Labour Law (Federal Law No. 8 of 1980). One of the most monumental reforms was the complete harmonization of end-of-service gratuity calculations across all contract types.

Abolishment of the Historical Resignation Penalty

Under the repealed 1980 law, employees who resigned under an unlimited contract suffered severe statutory deductions (receiving only 1/3 for 1–3 years or 2/3 for 3–5 years).

Under Article 51 of Decree-Law No. 33/2021, these penalty tiers have been totally eliminated. Whether an employee resigns or is terminated by the employer, their gratuity entitlement is 100% identical, provided they have completed at least one continuous year of service.

UAE Statutory Gratuity Formula (Article 51)
Daily Basic Wage = Monthly Basic Salary / 30
Years 1 to 5: Accrual = Daily Wage × 21 × Service Years (Max 5)
Years 6 Onwards: Accrual = Daily Wage × 30 × (Total Service Years - 5)
Statutory Maximum Cap = 24 × Monthly Basic Salary

Frequently Asked Questions

When must the employer disburse terminal dues in the UAE?
Under Article 53 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33/2021, the employer must settle all employee entitlements (including gratuity and untaken leave balance) within fourteen (14) days from the employment contract termination date.
