Statutory Mathematical Formula & Computation Mechanics

Effective February 2, 2022, Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 entirely replaced the previous 1980 Labour Law (Federal Law No. 8 of 1980). One of the most monumental reforms was the complete harmonization of end-of-service gratuity calculations across all contract types.

Abolishment of the Historical Resignation Penalty

Under the repealed 1980 law, employees who resigned under an unlimited contract suffered severe statutory deductions (receiving only 1/3 for 1–3 years or 2/3 for 3–5 years).

Under Article 51 of Decree-Law No. 33/2021, these penalty tiers have been totally eliminated. Whether an employee resigns or is terminated by the employer, their gratuity entitlement is 100% identical, provided they have completed at least one continuous year of service.

Frequently Asked Questions

MoHRE Wage Protection System (WPS) Severance Clearance & Dispute Protocols

In the United Arab Emirates, terminal severance settlement is formally monitored through the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE) Wage Protection System (WPS). Employers are statutory obligated to process final gratuity and accrued leave payouts within fourteen calendar days of the formal contract termination date under Article 53 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021.

Failure to disburse terminal benefits within this statutory timeframe triggers administrative blockages on the establishment's MoHRE portal, preventing the entity from issuing new work permits or renewing existing visas. If an employer disputes the computed basic wage or service tenure, either party may file a formal labor complaint through the MoHRE smart application or toll-free support line.

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2023 amending the dispute resolution framework, MoHRE holds executive authority to issue final binding decisions on claims not exceeding 50,000 AED. For claims exceeding this threshold where amicable settlement fails within twenty-one days, MoHRE refers the case directly to the competent Labour Court, accompanied by an official ministry summary memorandum detailing wage records and statutory calculations.

Expatriate End-of-Service Currency Repatriation & Banking Clearance

Expatriate professionals repatriating terminal end-of-service funds from the UAE benefit from an entirely open capital account regime enforced by the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE). Severance payouts transferred in UAE Dirhams (AED) are fully convertible into major global currencies without foreign exchange controls or outbound remittance withholding taxes.

However, expatriates holding active personal loans, credit cards, or auto financing must coordinate with their local retail bank prior to final settlement. When an employer tags a final salary deposit as "End of Service Benefit" in the WPS batch file, UAE commercial banks automatically place a temporary security hold on the account to offset outstanding liabilities.

To expedite account unfreezing and ensure seamless wire transfers to home country accounts, exiting workers should request an official liability letter or bank clearance certificate at least ten business days before their visa cancellation appointment. Once outstanding debt is settled or rescheduled, the bank releases the residual severance capital for international wire transfer.

Statutory Basic Wage vs Gross Salary Definition in UAE Labour Courts

A central point of dispute in terminal severance calculations across the UAE involves the precise legal distinction between basic wage and total gross salary. Under Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, end-of-service gratuity is calculated strictly on the employee's basic wage, defined as the contractual compensation stipulated in the registered MoHRE contract excluding all allowances and supplemental benefits.

Housing allowances, transportation subsidies, telephone allowances, performance commissions, and annual airfare stipends are statutorily excluded from the gratuity accrual formula. However, UAE labor courts have repeatedly ruled that if an employer artificially depresses the contractual basic wage to an unrealistic fraction of total remuneration to evade severance liabilities, judicial authorities have the discretion to reclassify regular fixed allowances as part of the core basic wage for gratuity determination.

Can an employer in UAE deduct visa costs from final gratuity?
Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33/2021 Article 6, employers are strictly prohibited from charging or deducting visa, recruitment, or residency processing costs from employee compensation or end-of-service gratuity settlements under any contractual pretext.
Does an employee receive gratuity if they resign before completing one year in UAE?
Under Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33/2021, workers who complete less than one continuous year of service are not entitled to end-of-service gratuity, regardless of whether separation occurs via resignation or employer termination.
Are housing and transport allowances included in UAE gratuity calculations?
Under Article 51 Clause 2, gratuity calculation is strictly based upon the employee's last drawn basic wage. Variable allowances including fixed housing, utility subsidies, and transport stipends are excluded from the statutory severance base.
What is the statutory deadline for employers to pay final gratuity in UAE?
Under Article 53 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33/2021, employers must disburse all accrued remuneration, untaken annual leave balance, and statutory gratuity entitlements within fourteen (14) calendar days from the official contract termination date.
How does the optional UAE workplace savings scheme replace cash gratuity?
Under Cabinet Resolution No. 96/2023, employers enrolled in approved alternative savings funds contribute monthly basic pay percentages (5.83% for first 5 years, 8.33% thereafter), exempting the enterprise from traditional defined-benefit lump-sum gratuity liabilities upon departure.
