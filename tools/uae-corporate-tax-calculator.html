UAE Corporate Tax Calculator: 9% Statutory Rate & SBR Relief

Direct Legal Rule: Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 47/2022, taxable net business profit is taxed at 0% up to AED 375,000 and 9% on any profit exceeding AED 375,000. Eligible businesses with gross annual revenue under AED 3,000,000 may elect for Small Business Relief (SBR) paying zero tax.

UAE Corporate Tax Architecture: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022

Effective for financial years commencing on or after June 1, 2023, the United Arab Emirates enacted a headline 9% Corporate Tax regime codified under Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022. Designed to maintain the nation's competitive standing while aligning with the OECD Base Erosion and Profit Shifting (BEPS Pillar Two) framework, the UAE corporate tax system introduces clear tiers.

The Headline Two-Tier Rate Structure

Article 3 of the Corporate Tax Law establishes a deliberate progressive relief threshold:

  • Taxable Net Profits up to AED 375,000: Taxed at 0%. This generous threshold shields micro-enterprises and freelancers from any federal tax burden.
  • Taxable Net Profits Exceeding AED 375,000: Taxed at 9% exclusively on the amount exceeding the AED 375,000 threshold.
UAE Corporate Tax Statutory Formula
If Taxable Profit ≤ AED 375,000 → Tax = AED 0
If Taxable Profit > AED 375,000 → Tax = (Taxable Profit - AED 375,000) × 9%
Effective Tax Rate = (Total Tax Payable / Total Taxable Profit) × 100

Small Business Relief (SBR) Under Ministerial Decision No. 73 of 2023

To support entrepreneurship, the Ministry of Finance issued Ministerial Decision No. 73 of 2023 introducing Small Business Relief (SBR):

Any resident taxable business whose gross turnover does not exceed AED 3,000,000 in the relevant tax period may elect for SBR. Upon election, the business is treated as having no taxable income for that tax period, reducing corporate tax liability to AED 0 regardless of profit margin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Free Zone companies exempt from UAE Corporate Tax?
Free Zone companies can benefit from a 0% corporate tax rate on "Qualifying Income" provided they qualify as a Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP), meet adequate substance requirements, and do not elect to be subject to regular corporate tax.
