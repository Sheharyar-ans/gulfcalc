Statutory Architecture of Bahrain LMRA Fees and Levies

The recruitment, regulation, and maintenance of expatriate human capital in the Kingdom of Bahrain is centralized under the Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA), established pursuant to Law No. 19 of 2006 regarding the Regulation of the Labour Market. The LMRA operates as an autonomous public institution executing workforce oversight in direct coordination with the Nationality, Passports and Residence Affairs (NPRA) and the Ministry of Labour.

Bahrain employment cost structure diverges from traditional regional visa frameworks by establishing a dual fee mechanism: a statutory upfront permit issuance fee covering multi-agency clearances, alongside recurring monthly employment levies assessed per active foreign worker on the commercial registration (CR). Navigating these multi-agency tariffs ensures precise financial budgeting for domestic enterprises and multinational operations.

Deconstruction of the Standard 2-Year Commercial Work Permit (172 BHD)

When an enterprise secures a standard two-year work permit for an expatriate professional, the total initial statutory fee of 172 BHD comprises the following inter-agency allocations:

Tiered Monthly LMRA Levies: 5 BHD vs. 10 BHD Tiers

In addition to upfront issuance costs, Bahrain enforces recurring monthly employment fees intended to regulate foreign worker ratios and fund national training initiatives managed by the Labour Fund (Tamkeen):

  • Micro and Small Business Tier (1 to 5 Workers): To stimulate startup capitalization, commercial registrations employing five or fewer foreign workers pay a concessional levy of 5 BHD per month per worker (aggregating to 60 BHD annually per worker).
  • Standard Commercial Enterprise Tier (6+ Workers): For the sixth foreign employee and every subsequent expatriate worker registered on the CR, the monthly levy increases to 10 BHD per month per worker (aggregating to 120 BHD annually per worker).
  • Flexi-Permit / Registered Worker Scheme: Self-sponsored foreign workers holding the Blue Card or operating through accredited registration centers pay a flat monthly regulatory fee of 15 BHD directly to the LMRA.

Synergy with SIO Expatriate End of Service Funds

In addition to LMRA levies, Bahraini employers must comply with Social Insurance Organisation (SIO) reforms under Ministerial Decision No. 109 of 2023. Employers remit direct monthly end of service contributions into the national expatriate protection fund, calculated on basic wages pursuant to Article 116 of Labour Law No. 36 of 2012. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 2-year commercial work permit cost in Bahrain? +
Under LMRA schedules, issuing or renewing a standard two-year commercial work permit costs 172 BHD per expatriate worker. This includes 100 BHD for the LMRA permit, 10 BHD for the NPRA residency visa, 12 BHD for administrative services, and 50 BHD for basic primary healthcare coverage under the Ministry of Health.
What is the monthly LMRA fee for commercial enterprises in Bahrain? +
For small and micro commercial registrations (CRs) employing up to five foreign workers, the statutory monthly LMRA fee is 5 BHD per worker. From the sixth foreign employee onwards, the monthly levy increases to 10 BHD per worker per month.
Can an employer deduct monthly LMRA fees from an employee salary? +
No. Under Bahrain Labour Law No. 36 of 2012 and LMRA Law No. 19 of 2006, the employer is legally obligated to bear all recruitment costs, initial permit fees, and monthly LMRA levies. Deducting government employment fees from an expatriate salary is an explicit statutory violation.
What is the monthly fee for a registered worker (Flexi-Permit) in Bahrain? +
Under the registered worker framework operated through accredited labour registration centers, a self-sponsored foreign worker pays a monthly statutory administrative levy of 15 BHD, alongside periodic medical insurance and permit maintenance tariffs.
Are domestic workers subject to monthly LMRA levies in Bahrain? +
No. Domestic workers (such as housemaids, private drivers, and home cooks) sponsored by individual Bahraini or resident households are exempt from the monthly 5 BHD or 10 BHD commercial LMRA levy. Sponsors pay only the one-time permit issuance and renewal fee.
