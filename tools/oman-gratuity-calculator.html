Oman Labour Law Reform: The Transition from RD 35/2003 to RD 53/2023

On July 25, 2023, His Majesty Sultan Haitham bin Tarik issued Royal Decree No. 53/2023, promulgating the new Labour Law of the Sultanate of Oman and officially repealing the longstanding Royal Decree No. 35/2003. This legislative overhaul introduced profound improvements for expatriate workers, particularly regarding terminal severance accrual.

The New Flat 30-Day Accrual Standard

Under the old framework (RD 35/2003), non-Omani employees received 15 days of basic wage per year for the first three continuous years of service, and 30 days per year for each subsequent year.

Under Royal Decree 53/2023 (Article 61), this bifurcation has been eliminated. Expatriate employees now accrue a flat one full month's basic salary (30 days' wage) for each completed year from year one. Fractions of a year are calculated pro-rata on the basis of the final basic wage.

End of Service Gratuity = Last Drawn Monthly Basic Wage × (Years + [Months / 12])

Frequently Asked Questions

Are housing and transport allowances included in Oman gratuity?
No. Article 61 explicitly benchmarks the calculation against the worker's latest Basic Wage (Al-Ajr Al-Asasi), unlike Saudi Arabia which utilizes the comprehensive Actual Wage.
Does resignation reduce Oman end of service gratuity?
Under Royal Decree 53/2023, non-Omani workers completing their lawful notice period under an indefinite or fixed contract are entitled to their accrued gratuity without arbitrary statutory penalty tiers.
