Oman Labour Law Reform: The Transition from RD 35/2003 to RD 53/2023

On July 25, 2023, His Majesty Sultan Haitham bin Tarik issued Royal Decree No. 53/2023, promulgating the new Labour Law of the Sultanate of Oman and officially repealing the longstanding Royal Decree No. 35/2003. This legislative overhaul introduced profound improvements for expatriate workers, particularly regarding terminal severance accrual.

The New Flat 30-Day Accrual Standard

Under the old framework (RD 35/2003), non-Omani employees received 15 days of basic wage per year for the first three continuous years of service, and 30 days per year for each subsequent year.

Under Royal Decree 53/2023 (Article 61), this bifurcation has been eliminated. Expatriate employees now accrue a flat one full month's basic salary (30 days' wage) for each completed year from year one. Fractions of a year are calculated pro-rata on the basis of the final basic wage.

End of Service Gratuity = Last Drawn Monthly Basic Wage × (Years + [Months / 12])

Frequently Asked Questions

How did Royal Decree 53/2023 change end-of-service calculations in Oman?
Under Article 61 of Oman Royal Decree No. 53/2023, gratuity accrues at a flat rate of one full month basic wage (30 days) per year from year one onwards, completely replacing the former split formula of 15 days.
Is Oman gratuity calculated on basic salary or gross remuneration?
Under Article 61 of Royal Decree No. 53/2023, gratuity computation is determined strictly by the employee last drawn basic wage. Supplemental allowances such as housing, utility, and conveyance allowances are legally excluded.
What is the minimum service period required to qualify for gratuity in Oman?
Under Article 61 of Oman Labour Law, expatriate employees must complete at least one continuous year of employment with the employer to qualify for statutory end-of-service gratuity entitlements.
Does an expatriate worker in Oman lose gratuity rights upon voluntary resignation?
Under Royal Decree No. 53/2023, expatriate employees who complete one continuous year of service retain full gratuity entitlements upon resignation, provided they comply with statutory notice obligations specified in Article 43.
How does the Oman Social Protection Fund impact expatriate gratuity payouts?
Under Oman Royal Decree No. 52/2023 (Social Protection Law), private enterprises are transitioning toward monthly savings contributions administered by the state fund, modernizing terminal benefit security for foreign nationals across the Sultanate.
