1. Statutory Foundations of the 10-Year Property Investor Visa

In accordance with Cabinet Resolution No. 65 of 2022 on the Executive Regulations of Federal Decree-Law No. 29 of 2021 regarding the Entry and Residence of Foreigners, real estate investors are entitled to secure a renewable 10-year residency visa without a local employer sponsor.

The benchmark for real estate qualification is set at an aggregate valuation of at least 2,000,000 AED. This title can consist of residential or commercial properties located within designated freehold investment zones across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, or other emirates. An investor is entitled to aggregate multiple titles (such as two one-bedroom apartments valued at 1,000,000 AED each) to satisfy the statutory threshold.

2. Removal of the Minimum Down Payment Requirement

Historically, investors purchasing mortgaged properties were required to prove a minimum cash equity contribution of at least 1,000,000 AED before immigration authorities would accept their Golden Visa application. In early 2024, the Dubai Land Department and the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA) formally eliminated this restriction.

Under current legal standards, as long as the purchase price or official valuation on the Title Deed or Oqood contract meets or exceeds 2,000,000 AED, the property qualifies for the Golden Visa regardless of the mortgage equity ratio, subject to presenting a standard bank No Objection Certificate (NOC).

3. Full Accounting of Real Estate Acquisition Costs

When evaluating property acquisitions in the Emirate of Dubai, investors must account for statutory closing fees that accompany the purchase:

  • Dubai Land Department (DLD) Transfer Fee: 4.0% of the purchase value, plus a 580 AED administrative fee.
  • Registration Trustee Fee: 4,000 AED plus 5% VAT (totaling 4,200 AED) for properties valued at 500,000 AED or above.
  • Real Estate Agency Commission: Standard 2.0% of the asset price plus 5% VAT.
  • Golden Visa Processing Costs: Approximately 9,860 AED covering pre-approval nomination, entry permit, VIP medical screening, and 10-year Emirates ID card issuance.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Cabinet Resolution No. 65 of 2022: Real Estate Investor Visa Criteria

Under Cabinet Resolution No. 65 of 2022 issuing the Executive Regulations of the Federal Decree-Law on the Entry and Residence of Foreigners, the United Arab Emirates established a prestigious 10-year self-sponsored Golden Visa for real estate investors. To qualify, foreign buyers must acquire one or more residential properties with a combined purchase value of not less than two million AED (2,000,000 AED).

In 2024, the UAE officially abolished the historical requirement for a minimum upfront cash payment of one million AED. Under current regulations, investors purchasing off-plan or mortgaged properties qualify for the 10-year visa, provided the total property valuation documented on the official title deed or initial sales contract (Oqood) exceeds the statutory two million AED threshold.

The Golden Visa grants complete independence from employer sponsorship, permits remaining outside the UAE for extended periods exceeding six months without visa invalidation, and allows sponsoring spouses, children of any age, and domestic staff under unified family sponsorship rules.

Comprehensive Purchase Cost Stack: DLD, Registration & Legal Fees

Investors evaluating the Golden Visa property route must account for mandatory statutory closing costs beyond the real estate purchase price. In Dubai, these fees include:

  • Dubai Land Department (DLD) Transfer Fee: 4% of property purchase price plus 580 AED administrative title deed fee.
  • Property Registration Trustee Fee: 4,000 AED plus 5% VAT (for properties valued at 500,000 AED or above).
  • Mortgage Registration Fee (if leveraged): 0.25% of the total loan amount plus 290 AED administrative fee.
  • Golden Visa Application & Medical Fitness: Approximately 8,500 to 10,000 AED per primary investor, covering VIP medical testing, Emirates ID issuance, and visa stamping.

Accounting for these statutory outlays ensures investors maintain adequate liquidity when completing real estate acquisitions for Golden Visa qualification.

Off-Plan & Mortgaged Property Golden Visa Qualification Guidelines

Under updated regulatory directives issued by the Dubai Land Department (DLD) and GDRFA, property investors acquiring off-plan real estate can qualify for the 10-year Golden Visa residency once the total contractual purchase value equals or exceeds two million AED, even if the property is currently under construction.

The applicant must present an official initial contract of sale (Oqood) issued by the Dubai Land Department, along with an official developer account statement verifying that scheduled instalment payments are in good standing. For mortgaged completed properties, the title deed reflecting a purchase valuation of two million AED or above satisfies the statutory requirement, regardless of the outstanding bank mortgage balance.

What is the minimum real estate purchase price for a 10-year UAE Golden Visa?
Under Cabinet Resolution No. 65/2022, property investors must purchase one or more real estate units with a total aggregate purchase value of at least AED 2,000,000 registered on the title deed.
Can an off-plan property qualify for the UAE Real Estate Golden Visa?
Off-plan properties qualify provided the total contractual value is at least AED 2,000,000 and the project is registered with the Dubai Land Department (DLD) through an approved master developer with Oqood registration.
Is a mortgaged property eligible for the 2 million AED UAE Golden Visa?
Mortgaged properties are fully eligible if the total registered purchase price is at least AED 2,000,000, and local banks provide an official NOC letter affirming statutory equity conditions.
What mandatory government fees are required to process a property Golden Visa?
Mandatory government processing costs include Dubai Land Department nomination fees (~AED 4,020), medical fitness examination (~AED 750), Emirates ID issuance, and visa stamping fees across the 10-year residency.
Can family members and domestic staff be sponsored under the UAE Property Golden Visa?
Under Executive Regulations, the Golden Visa holder can sponsor their spouse, children of any age without upper age restrictions, and an unlimited number of domestic support staff under valid residency contracts.
