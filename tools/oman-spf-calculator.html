Oman Social Protection Fund (SPF) Calculator: Royal Decree No. 52/2023

Under Oman Social Protection Law Royal Decree No. 52 of 2023, Omani private sector employees contribute 8.0% of their contributory wage towards retirement and job security, while employers contribute 13.5% encompassing retirement, work injury, job security, and mandatory statutory maternity and sick leave insurance branches.

Statutory Architecture of the Oman Social Protection Fund

The promulgation of Royal Decree No. 52 of 2023 enacting the Social Protection Law stands as the most comprehensive overhaul of social security legislation in the history of the Sultanate of Oman. The statute consolidated eleven fragmented pension and retirement funds (including the Public Authority for Social Insurance, the Civil Service Pension Fund, and military security schemes) into a single, unified institutional authority: the Social Protection Fund (SPF).

Effective January 1, 2024, the new regulatory scheme fundamentally aligned private sector employee benefits with public service standards, establishing harmonized contribution formulas, standardized retirement eligibility milestones, integrated parental leave provisions, and universal coverage for occupational hazards. Understanding the exact mathematical mechanics governing payroll deductions is essential for corporate finance controllers, HR directors, and Omani citizens planning long-term pension accruals.

Statutory Wage Boundaries: Floor and Ceiling

Under Oman labor and social protection decrees, the contributory salary subjected to social insurance deductions is bounded by strict statutory floors and ceilings:

  • Statutory Minimum Wage Floor: In accordance with Ministerial Decision No. 235/2023, the baseline minimum salary for Omani nationals in the private sector is set at 325 OMR per month. SPF contributions cannot be evaluated on any amount below this legal minimum.
  • Statutory Maximum Contributory Ceiling: Under Article 74 of the Social Protection Executive Regulations, the maximum wage ceiling subject to SPF percentage deductions is capped at exactly 3,000 OMR per month. For executive personnel whose basic compensation and allowances exceed 3,000 OMR, all contribution rates freeze at the 3,000 OMR boundary.

Treatment of Expatriate Personnel Under SPF

For foreign expatriate workers employed in the Sultanate, the Social Protection Law introduces a fundamental shift. Employers must remit an ongoing 1.0% statutory contribution based on the expatriate monthly basic wage to fund the Work Injury and Occupational Diseases branch. This grants expatriate employees comprehensive medical coverage and statutory disability compensations in the event of workplace accidents.

Foreign workers do not pay into the 7.5% retirement pension or 0.5% job security funds. Instead, expatriate end of service benefits continue to accrue under Oman Labour Law Royal Decree No. 53 of 2023 at the flat standard rate of 30 days basic salary per year of service, calculated through our verified Oman Gratuity Calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the employee deduction percentage under Oman SPF in 2026? +
Under Oman Social Protection Law Royal Decree No. 52 of 2023, an Omani citizen employed in the private or public sector incurs an aggregate monthly deduction of 8.0% from their contributory wage. This consists of 7.5% allocated to the Old Age, Disability, and Death (Retirement) branch and 0.5% allocated to the Job Security (Unemployment) scheme.
What is the employer contribution rate under the Oman Social Protection Fund? +
Private sector employers in Oman contribute a combined 13.5% of an Omani employee contributory wage to the SPF. This incorporates 11.0% for retirement insurance, 1.0% for work injury and occupational diseases insurance, 0.5% for job security insurance, and 1.0% for mandatory maternity and sick leave allowances.
Does the Oman Social Protection Fund apply to expatriate employees? +
Yes, partially. Under Royal Decree No. 52 of 2023, private sector employers must pay 1.0% of an expatriate employee monthly basic wage to the SPF to cover the Work Injury and Occupational Diseases branch. Expatriates do not pay retirement pension deductions, but remain entitled to statutory end of service gratuity under Royal Decree No. 53 of 2023.
What is the statutory wage ceiling for SPF contributions in Oman? +
The statutory contributory wage ceiling under the Social Protection Law is capped at exactly 3,000 OMR per month. Any portion of an employee monthly basic salary and qualifying allowances exceeding 3,000 OMR is exempt from social insurance percentage deductions.
How does the SPF handle maternity and paternity leave insurance in Oman? +
The Social Protection Fund centralized maternity and parental allowances under a unified social insurance branch funded entirely by a 1.0% employer contribution. Working mothers receive 98 days of fully paid maternity leave paid directly by the SPF rather than the individual employer, while fathers receive 7 days of statutory paid paternity leave.
