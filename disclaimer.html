3. Contractual Variations and Exclusions

Individual employment contracts, collective bargaining agreements, specialized free zone regulations (such as DIFC or ADGM in the UAE, or QFC in Qatar), and disciplinary history can substantially impact terminal benefits. In particular:

  • Employees enrolled in an alternative corporate gratuity savings scheme or state pension may be subject to separate contribution offset rules;
  • Gross misconduct or summary dismissal under statutory provisions (e.g., Article 80 in Saudi Arabia or Article 41 in Kuwait) can nullify terminal dues;
